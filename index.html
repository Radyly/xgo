CONTROL LANGUAGE

Designed for quick demonstrations and easy-to-remember gestures.

🤏
LEFT PINCH
Hold to activate

Hold thumb and index finger together on the left hand as a dead-man switch. Release = stop.

👆
RIGHT INDEX
Point to drive

Point away from you for forward, toward the floor for back, and to either side to turn.

✋
OPEN RIGHT PALM
Neutral / hold

Open your right hand or close it into a fist to pause movement, even while left pinch is held.

👐
BOTH PALMS OPEN
Emergency stop

Hold both palms open for one second to latch the emergency stop; resume from this page.
